USE CASE 01 — YOUR TOP PRODUCER

The listing launch, handled end to end

Before: One of your top producers signs a strong listing, then loses 4–6 hours to photos, copy, a page, social, and email — rebuilt from scratch, by whoever is free.

The system: Photos and address come to us. We build the premium listing page, launch copy, social posts, and sphere email, and stand up a Coming Soon waitlist where it applies.

Outcome: listing live in hours (under 24 hrs on Full), presented like the price deserves — and the producer spent the day selling.

USE CASE 02 — TEAM LEADER

The leader drowning in follow-up

Before: Every portal lead, website inquiry, and open-house signup routes through the team leader's phone. Leads wait hours. The leader is the switchboard.

The system: Quality Scorer scores every inquiry the moment it lands — fit, intent, recency, engagement, completeness, with plain-English reason codes — ranks it, enriches it with source, history and intent, and routes the follow-up to the right agent. A first touch inside 60 seconds is the floor. The leader gets one clean summary.

Outcome: no lead sits, conversion stops leaking to faster competitors, and the leader gets their evenings back.

USE CASE 03 — BROKERAGE OWNER

Recruiting producers, not chasing them

Before: The brokerage wants producing agents but its recruiting page and content look like an afterthought — so recruits choose teams that look bigger.

The system: Recruiting page rebuilt, agent spotlights, and market-authority content running continuously, so targets have seen the brokerage winning for weeks before any conversation.

Outcome: recruiting conversations that start warm. One recruited producer at 12 sides/yr ≈ $120K+ GCI.

USE CASE 04 — WHOLE TEAM

The dormant database, reactivated

Before: Thousands of past clients, dead leads, and sphere contacts — already paid for — get a holiday text and nothing else.

The system: We segment the CRM, write outreach in the leader's voice, run the campaign, and route every reply to the right agent with context.

Outcome: conversations and listings from contacts you already own — the cheapest inventory in your market.

USE CASE 05 — TOP LISTING

The coming-soon funnel

Before: A signature listing sits silent for two weeks before hitting the MLS, then launches cold.

The system: A Coming Soon page builds a buyer waitlist pre-MLS; the seller watches demand form in real time; launch day starts with showings lined up.

Outcome: leverage for your seller and momentum on day one — not a cold launch and a prayer.
