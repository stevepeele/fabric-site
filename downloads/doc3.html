Fabric. — Use Cases, By Role

fabric.stevepeeleii.com · Market Movers — Cincinnati · Fall 2026 · Brief v2

Seven seats. Seven different days. Same layer underneath. How a team actually uses Fabric, role by role. Modules are shown as capabilities — pricing lives on the Stages & Pricing one-pager.

SEAT 01 — TEAM LEADER

You stop being the switchboard. You run the tower.

Before: Your phone is the CRM. Every lead, listing, and problem routes through you. Evenings are follow-up audit.

The day: 7:30 Live Board brief — overnight leads scored and routed, listings launched, hand-raisers surfaced. 9:00 Cascade queue — approve once, the whole team posts. Midday: only judgment calls reach you. 17:00 Dardo roll-up — every field recap, 2-minute retro.

Runs underneath: Oscar (engine) · Quality Scorer · Dardo leader roll-up · Cascade approvals · Nurture Engine signal summary

Outcome: ~40 interruptions a day → a decision queue of five or fewer.
SEAT 02 — LISTING AGENT

Sign it Tuesday. Launched before you’ve finished telling the seller.

Before: Sign a listing, lose 4–6 hours to photos, copy, a page, social, email — rebuilt from scratch every time.

The day: Tue: listing signed, one tap in Dardo. Wed 09:00: photos land, Fabric builds the page, copy, sphere email. Wed PM: live everywhere via Syndicate + Cascade. Pre-MLS: the Coming Soon page builds the waitlist.

Runs underneath: Listing Launch · Syndicate · Cascade · Coming Soon Funnels · Dardo capture

Outcome: Live in hours, not days. 4–6 hours per listing, back.
SEAT 03 — BUYER’S AGENT

Your job is the houses and the people. Not the paperwork after.

Before: Showings all day, admin all night. Notes scattered, follow-ups drafted from memory at 10pm.

The day: Morning: route + showings on one board, context attached. In the field: one tap per house in Dardo. Evening: your brief is waiting — recaps written, follow-ups drafted in your voice. At close: You Found Home takes the next 60 days.

Runs underneath: Dardo (field seat) · Oscar routing · Showing Ops · You Found Home handoff

Outcome: ~90 minutes of after-showing admin → a 10-minute review.
SEAT 04 — TC / ADMIN / OPS MANAGER

Nothing reaches a client unqualified. Nothing lives only in your head.

Before: You’re the human router and the human reminder system. Every file, deadline, and status question is yours.

The day: Morning: one exception queue — Oscar has already qualified everything inbound, with plain-English reason codes. Midday: deadlines and file status on the Live Board, visible to everyone. Always: documents flow through the tools you already use.

Runs underneath: Oscar qualification gate · Quality Scorer completeness + reason codes · Dardo recaps · your existing stack

Outcome: Inbox-as-system → exception-only. The operation survives your day off.
SEAT 05 — ISA / LEAD HANDLER

You only talk to people who are ready to talk.

Before: A chronological inbox where the loudest and the latest win. Hot leads wait behind junk. Every conversation starts blank.

The day: All day: a ranked stack, not an inbox — every lead scored on fit, intent, recency, engagement, completeness. Each card arrives enriched. First touch inside 60 seconds is the floor. Hand-raisers feed in live from signal monitoring.

Runs underneath: Quality Scorer · Nurture Engine hand-raiser feed · Oscar routing

Outcome: No lead sits. Your calls start at the top of the stack, every time.
SEAT 06 — BROKERAGE OWNER

Scale the brokerage without becoming the bottleneck at scale.

Before: Growth means more chaos in more places. Recruiting is a scramble when someone remembers. You see problems weeks after they start.

The day: Weekly: leadership board — every team, every pipeline, one view. Recruiting, running: Build an Empire identifies your next producers from your own deal-side data, ranked and approached discreetly — you control every approach. Always: your brokerage looks like the place winners work.

Runs underneath: Build an Empire · Oscar · Cascade (brokerage-wide) · leadership roll-up

Outcome: One recruited producer ≈ $120K+ GCI a year. Recruiting becomes a system, not an event.
SEAT 07 — PAST CLIENT — THE HOMEOWNER

You found home. We never left.

Before: The industry standard: closing day is the last thoughtful contact. Then a holiday text, forever.

The day: Day 15: settle-in check-in — anything unresolved surfaces privately to the agent. Day 30: value only, no pitch. Day 45: the referral path opens as a game. Day 60: the review ask, earned, routed everywhere. Then nurture keeps the relationship warm for years.

Runs underneath: You Found Home · Nurture Engine continuity · Oscar

Outcome: A set of referrals is the designed outcome — not one lucky intro.

The pattern: Different problems. Same fix. Owners don’t chase the symptom — they fix the system it came from. That’s the job we take, permanently.
