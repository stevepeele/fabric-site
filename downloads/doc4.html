The three leaks that cost the most

  • Speed-to-lead (5 hrs 40 mins in our test). At a $400K average sale, one side is $10,000–$12,000 GCI. Every inquiry answered hours late is a coin flip you didn't have to take.
  • The silent database. A team at this volume typically holds several thousand past clients and sphere contacts. With no reactivation system, that inventory compounds for competitors instead.
  • Looking smaller than you are. $118M of production presented like a boutique startup costs listings at the kitchen table and recruits before the first conversation.

First 14 days — the fix list (what the 14-Day Proof Sprint would do)

  • Days 1–3: Homepage + team leader page reimagined around the real promise and proof — nothing ripped out; profiles (Google, Zillow, social) aligned.
  • Days 4–7: Agent pages reimagined for all producers; Listing Launch Kit live — page, copy, social, email per listing. Checkpoint 1 (20 min, Day 7).
  • Days 8–11: Quality Scorer wired — every inquiry scored on fit, intent, recency, engagement and completeness, ranked, enriched and routed to the right agent; the database groomed, enriched and segmented.
  • Days 12–14: Recruiting page live; first nurture email and content cadence running. Checkpoint 2 (20 min, Day 14) — Proof live, nothing broken.

Verdict (sample): Strong production, weak orchestration. The production has already been earned — the presentation and follow-up systems haven't caught up. That's the cheapest kind of gap to close, and the fastest to pay back: one accelerated closing ($10–12K GCI) covers a month of Full ($3,950/mo), and then some.
