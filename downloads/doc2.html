The math — what is one accelerated commission worth?

At a $400,000 average sale, one side at 2.5% = $10,000 and at 3.0% = $12,000 in gross commission. One accelerated closing ($10–12K GCI) covers a month of Full ($3,950/mo), and then some. One retained or recruited producer doing 12 sides a year is roughly $120K+ in GCI. Before splits/fees; your numbers vary — the point holds at almost any split.
