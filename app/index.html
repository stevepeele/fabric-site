  • Who it's for: independent brokers, team leaders, and firms with real production — $50M+ teams, $250M+ brokerages — that are proving they can scale and are willing to invest in scaling.

  • Weekly: nurture email + content cadence out the door, no chasing.
  • Monthly: the database groomed and enriched again + a plain-English report — what shipped, replies, appointments set.
